About STRIWA

Where potential meets opportunity

STRIWA is a proposed women-empowerment initiative that builds a supportive and inclusive ecosystem where women can express themselves, develop skills, build professional networks and grow personally and professionally.

Women contribute greatly to education, entrepreneurship, technology and leadership, yet unequal opportunities, limited mentorship and weak networks still hold many back. STRIWA goes beyond financial independence — it focuses on confidence, education, self-expression, skills, leadership and social participation.

Every woman deserves to be heard, valued and empowered to shape her own future.

Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Purpose

✦

Vision

An inclusive society where every woman has the confidence, freedom, support and opportunities to pursue her dreams and realize her full potential.

✦

Mission

To empower women by connecting their aspirations with opportunities through community, mentorship, knowledge sharing, skill development and visibility for their ideas.

✦

Purpose

To bridge the gap between what women aspire to achieve and the resources, connections and opportunities that help turn ambition into reality.
